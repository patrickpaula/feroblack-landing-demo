THE CONCEPT

What are pheromones?

Pheromones are chemical substances used for communication between individuals of the same species. In the animal world, this is well documented.

In humans, science still debates the role of these substances. That's why FERO BLACK is positioned as a perfume with pheromones, with no promise of results: what we deliver is fragrance, ritual, and presence.

Is it really a pheromone perfume?

Yes, it's a perfume with pheromones. Science still debates their role in humans, so we don't promise an effect on other people: what we deliver is fragrance, ritual, and presence.

Does the perfume guarantee attraction?

No. The experience with fragrances is personal and results vary. There's no guarantee of any effect on others.

How should I use it and how long does it last?
